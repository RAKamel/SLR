The project repository is available at \url{https://github.com/RAKamel/SLR}. It contains the study bibliography under \texttt{paper/}, the extraction matrix at \texttt{data/extraction/extraction.xlsx}, and the screening corpus at \texttt{data/screening/corpus.xlsx}. The corpus workbook includes the duplicate-removal log. Dated search exports and search strings are stored under \texttt{data/raw/}; screening criteria, retrieval records, and retraction checks are under \texttt{data/screening/}. Reusable processing, agreement, PRISMA, and table-generation scripts are provided under \texttt{scripts/}. PRISMA counts are recorded in \texttt{data/prisma\_counts.json}.

The extraction and coding records contain dated provenance and verification notes. Many entries remain AI-prefilled or only partly verified; repository availability must not be interpreted as certification of their correctness. Complete-paper verification and author confirmation remain in progress, and the public repository may precede the latest working corrections. Publisher full texts are excluded from redistribution. Availability claims concerning included studies refer to their own artifacts and are qualified separately in the extraction matrix.
